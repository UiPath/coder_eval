# Code of Conduct

coder_eval follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while being precise about technical issues.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Help make the project easier for new contributors.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, customer data, or private operational data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or escalate to the project owner when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers at **coder-eval@uipath.com**. Do not open public issues for sensitive conduct reports.
